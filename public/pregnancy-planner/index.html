Introduction

Welcome to Your Pregnancy Journey

Congratulations. Whatever brought you here — a long-awaited positive test, a happy surprise, a second or third journey — this planner is a quiet place to keep it all together.

Inside you will find a page for every week from one to forty, with a short and simple look at how your baby is growing, space for appointments and questions, gentle reminders of what you might want to prepare, and plenty of room for the parts worth remembering.

There is no right way to use it. Fill in every field or only the ones that matter to you. Print it, write in it, or keep it on a tablet. Skip weeks. Come back later. This is yours.

How to use this planner
01Start with your dates. Fill in the Important Dates page so everything else has an anchor.
02Follow along weekly. Each week has a development note, a to-do list, and space to journal.
03Track appointments. Use the tracker and the questions pages so nothing gets forgotten at a visit.
04Reflect monthly. Nine reflection pages give you a wider view of how things are changing.
05Prepare gently. Checklists for the nursery, hospital bag, and birth plan sit near the back.
06Finish with the arrival. The last pages are for the day you meet your baby.
