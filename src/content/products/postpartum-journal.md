The months after a birth come with almost no structure. Appointments are scattered, days blur, and the one book everyone gave you covers pregnancy and stops at the delivery.

This journal covers the part after. It is a place to keep the practical details of the first weeks and a place to write down how it actually went, which turns out to matter later.

## What's inside

- **Daily log pages** through the first twelve weeks — feeds, sleep, mood, and what happened
- **Weekly reflection pages** with room to write properly, not just fill boxes
- **Appointment tracker** for both of you, since there are two sets of follow-ups
- **Questions for my provider** pages
- **Visitor log** and a page for who offered what help, because in the moment you will not remember who said they would bring dinner
- **Meal train tracker**
- **Milestone and first-times pages**
- **Letters to the baby**
- **A "what I want to remember" page** for each month

## Who it's for

Anyone in the first few months at home. It is written for a person who is tired, so the pages are short and nothing needs to be filled in daily to still be useful.

## A note on scope

This is a journal, not a clinical tool. It does not screen for anything, interpret symptoms, or offer guidance about recovery or mental health. If something feels off, that is a conversation for your provider — and there is an appointment page for exactly that.

## Formats and printing

US Letter and A4, standard and fillable. Most people print this one and keep it somewhere reachable with one hand.
