## A Palm Springs wedding starts with a sense of place

A hand-drawn venue sketch gives this save the date its desert character without crowding out the information guests need. The illustration sets the scene; your names, wedding date and location make it personal. It is a starting point for couples who want a considered design and the flexibility to edit their own wording.

The listing photos show the design styled on paper and displayed digitally. This purchase is a **digital Canva template**, not a physical card, envelope or printing service.

## What you can personalize

**Edit the text:** replace the sample names, wedding date, location and wording on the back. Add your confirmed wedding website if you want guests to have a place to find travel information.

**Adjust fonts and colors:** the template uses elements available with a free Canva account. Keep the smaller information readable and give the design enough breathing room rather than adding every weekend detail to the front.

**Keep the illustration or request a new one:** changing the venue sketch to a drawing of your own venue is a custom design request. It is not included as a standard edit to this template. ## Save the date wording you can use

Keep the message short. The formal invitation will carry the ceremony time, full address and response instructions later.

> Save the date
>
> [Name] & [Name]
>
> [Month day, year]
>
> Palm Springs, California
>
> Formal invitation to follow
>
> [Wedding website, if ready]

If some guests will receive this before you have confirmed the venue, use the destination rather than an unconfirmed venue name. For more options, read the [wording and etiquette guide](/wording-etiquette/).

## Printing without the guesswork

Before choosing paper, confirm the final card size and the printer’s file requirements. Ask whether they need bleed and crop marks, then export accordingly. A printed proof is useful for checking text size, colors, trimming and how the front and back line up.

For a 5 × 7-inch card, choose envelopes specified to fit that finished size. If you print at home, check the printer’s supported paper weight before buying cardstock. If you use a print shop, send the exported file rather than a screenshot of Canva.

Printing costs vary by quantity, paper and provider. Compare the total cost of cards, envelopes and postage separately from the template price. Our [printing guide](/printing-guide/) covers paper, sizing and choosing a printing route.

## Planning a destination wedding mailing

Send your save the date once the wedding date and destination are confirmed, with enough lead time for your guests to make travel plans. Consider how far they are traveling and when accommodation information will be available rather than treating one mailing date as right for every wedding.

The save the date does not need a full itinerary. Use your wedding website for confirmed travel information and update it as arrangements are finalized.
