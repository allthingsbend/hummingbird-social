## Bring the desert setting into your invitation

The hand-drawn landscape is the defining feature of this Joshua Tree wedding invitation template. It gives guests a sense of the setting while the editable wording explains where to go, when to arrive, and what kind of celebration you are planning.

The design works for a desert wedding, an intimate outdoor ceremony, or an elopement celebration. Your wording should describe the actual event. A party after a private ceremony needs a different invitation from a ceremony where every recipient is attending.

## What is included in the digital download

- One editable Canva wedding invitation design.
- A 5x7-inch layout with the desert illustration shown in the gallery.
- PDF and JPEG export options for your finished invitation.
- The Canva Basics guide with video tutorials.

This purchase is for the digital template. Printing, envelopes, postage, and additional stationery are separate. A matching RSVP card or details card can be discussed through a [custom design request](/custom-design-requests/); they are not included automatically.

## Make the desert wedding invitation your own

Start with your names, date, ceremony time, and venue. Then add the reception details and dress code if they belong on the main invitation. The text and palette are editable in Canva, while the desert illustration remains the existing artwork.

Choose a text color with enough contrast against the background. A soft desert palette can look beautiful on screen, but very pale lettering may become hard to read in print. Preview the actual names and address before deciding on colors, and use the [how it works guide](/how-it-works/) if this is your first Canva template.

## Joshua Tree wedding invitation wording examples

Use these fictional examples as starting points and replace every bracketed detail.

### For a ceremony and reception

> Together with their families\
> Riley and Cameron\
> invite you to celebrate their wedding\
> [Day, month, date, year] at [Time]\
> [Venue name] · [City, State]\
> Dinner and dancing to follow\
> Details and RSVP: [Wedding website]

### For a celebration after an elopement

> We’re married!\
> Join Riley and Cameron\
> for a celebration of their elopement\
> [Day, month, date, year] at [Time]\
> [Venue name and location]\
> Please RSVP by [Date] at [Website]

Keep the main card focused on the invitation itself. Longer notes about accommodations, parking, accessibility, and the weekend schedule fit better on a details card or wedding website. Find more hosting and RSVP examples in the [wording and etiquette guide](/wording-etiquette/).

## Help guests prepare for an outdoor celebration

If the ceremony is outdoors, give guests useful information somewhere in your stationery or website. Explain the surface they will walk on, whether there is a shuttle, where to park, and whom to contact with access questions. Describe the dress code in plain language so guests can choose clothing and footwear confidently.

Use your confirmed venue instructions rather than general assumptions about Joshua Tree. A stationery design does not establish that a venue is within the national park, and any venue or permit arrangements need to be handled separately.

## Export and print the desert illustration

1. Proofread the names, date, time, and venue address inside Canva.
2. Ask your printer which PDF settings, bleed, and crop marks they require.
3. Export at the intended 5x7-inch size.
4. Print a single sample and check the drawing, text contrast, and trimming.
5. Confirm the card fits your envelope before placing the full order.

Matte or lightly textured paper is a useful starting point for a line illustration. Choose a stock your printer supports rather than relying on a paper weight alone. The [printing guide](/printing-guide/) explains the practical checks.

## Plan the rest of your desert stationery

Build a consistent suite by repeating your names, palette, and wording style across the pieces guests will actually use. Before ordering custom additions, list the information each piece needs to carry so the invitation does not repeat the entire weekend schedule.
