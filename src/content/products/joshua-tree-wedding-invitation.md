Desert weddings get one of two treatments in most template shops: a photograph of a cactus, or a minimalist arch with a single line drawing dropped in the corner. This one is a proper hand-drawn desert illustration built into the layout rather than placed on top of it.

## What you get

- An editable Canva template
- A print-ready 5x7 layout with bleed
- The Canva Basics guide with video tutorials
- Instant download after purchase

## Editing it

Everything text-based is editable — names, date, venue, ceremony and reception details, dress code line. The illustration stays as drawn, which is the part that makes it look designed rather than assembled.

## Printing this one

Line illustrations reward good paper. A matte or lightly textured cardstock around 110 lb keeps the linework crisp; very glossy stock flattens it. Details are in the [printing guide](/printing-guide/).

## Timing

Invitations go out six to eight weeks before a local wedding, and closer to three months for a destination one where guests are traveling. If you are sending save the dates first, the [wording and etiquette guide](/wording-etiquette/) has the full timeline.
