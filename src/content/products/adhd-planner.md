Most planners are designed by people who enjoy planners. They are dated, they are long, and they assume you will fill in every page. Miss four days and the book becomes a record of failure that you then avoid, which is how a fifty-dollar planner ends up in a drawer in March.

This one is built around the opposite assumption: that you will use it hard for a stretch, drop it, and come back. Everything is undated, every page is short, and nothing breaks if you skip a week.

## What's inside

- **Yearly calendar pages**, undated, for the handful of dates that actually need to live somewhere permanent
- **Monthly overview** pages
- **Weekly layouts** in two versions — one time-blocked, one list-based — because people split hard on which they can stand
- **Daily pages** with three priorities, not fifteen
- **Brain dump pages** for getting everything out of your head before you sort it
- **Task triage sheets** — a simple pass for sorting a dump into today, this week, and not now
- **Routine builders** for morning and evening
- **Habit trackers** built in short runs rather than full months
- **Project breakdown pages** for splitting one large vague thing into actual next steps
- **Waiting on** and **follow up** pages, for the things that stall out because they depend on somebody else

## The design choices

Three deliberate ones, and they are the reason it works when other planners have not:

**Undated throughout.** No wasted pages, no guilt from a gap.

**Short daily pages.** Whitespace on a planner page reads as an unfinished task. Less space to fill means the page actually gets finished.

**Print only what you use.** You are not committing to a bound book. Find the two layouts that fit and print those.

## A note on scope

This is an organizational planner. It makes no clinical claims and is not a treatment, therapy, or coaching tool.

## Formats and printing

US Letter and A4, standard and fillable. Because it is undated and print-what-you-need, a three-ring binder or a discbound cover works better than spiral binding here.
