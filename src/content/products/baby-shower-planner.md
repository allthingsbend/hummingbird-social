Hosting a baby shower is mostly logistics wearing a party hat. There is a guest list you did not write, a registry you did not build, a venue, a menu, a game or two, and a set of thank-you notes waiting on the other side.

This planner handles the hosting side of it, so the details stay in one place instead of across a group chat, a notes app, and three browser tabs.

## What's inside

- **An eight-week timeline** working backward from the shower date
- **Guest list tracker** with addresses, RSVPs, dietary notes, and gifts received
- **Budget worksheet**, including the awkward part — splitting costs between co-hosts
- **Menu and shopping lists**, separated into make-ahead and day-of
- **Games and activities pages** with setup notes and supply lists
- **Decor and supply checklist**
- **Day-of run sheet** — arrival, setup, food out, games, gifts, cleanup
- **Gift log** with a thank-you-note tracking column

## Who it's for

Whoever is hosting: a sister, a best friend, a mother-in-law, a group of coworkers splitting it three ways. It assumes you are not a professional event planner and that you have a job.

## Formats and printing

Four files — US Letter and A4, each standard and fillable. The gift log and the day-of run sheet are the two worth printing; everything else works fine on a screen.
