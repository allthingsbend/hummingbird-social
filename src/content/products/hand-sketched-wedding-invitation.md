## A venue illustration with room to breathe

The drawing gives this hand sketched wedding invitation its character. The surrounding layout stays restrained, leaving your names, ceremony details, and dress code easy to find. It suits couples who want their stationery to feel personal without filling every corner of the card.

Use the photo gallery to check the illustration and overall balance before choosing. This listing uses the venue drawing shown in the previews. If you want your own building, ceremony setting, or another meaningful place illustrated, start with a [custom design request](/custom-design-requests/) so the artwork can be discussed first.

## Personalize the invitation in a clear order

1. Open the template link supplied through your Etsy purchase.
2. Replace the names and hosting line first. Longer names may need different line breaks.
3. Enter the wedding date, ceremony time, venue name, and address.
4. Add reception information and a dress code where relevant.
5. Review the whole card at its final size, then export the file for your printer.

Treat the illustration as the visual anchor. Leave space around it and avoid shrinking all the text to fit extra paragraphs. If the address or reception details make the invitation crowded, a wedding website or separate details card can carry the longer explanation. The [how it works guide](/how-it-works/) explains the editing and download process.

## Formal wedding invitation wording to adapt

Start with the information guests need, then adjust the hosting line to fit your family and celebration. This fictional example shows one possible structure:

> Together with their families\
> Avery Morgan and Jordan Lee\
> request the pleasure of your company\
> at their wedding celebration\
> Saturday, the eighteenth of September\
> at four o’clock in the afternoon\
> [Venue name] · [City, State]\
> Reception to follow · Black tie

Write out a formal date if you like that style, but keep the time unambiguous. Only use “reception to follow” when it accurately describes the plan. For different hosts, reception locations, and dress codes, see the [wedding wording and etiquette guide](/wording-etiquette/).

## Print the fine linework with confidence

A subtle venue drawing needs a proof more than it needs a particular paper label. Try matte or lightly textured cardstock, then check the linework and the smallest text in ordinary light. Your printer’s supported paper options and file specifications should guide the final choice.

Export a PDF for printing and keep the intended 5x7-inch size. Ask whether the printer requires crop marks or bleed, and order one sample before approving the full run. If printing at home, check the printer’s cardstock limit and turn off automatic scaling that changes the finished size. More file and paper guidance is in the [printing guide](/printing-guide/).

## Before you place the full print order

- Confirm every name and the venue spelling with another person.
- Check the date against the day of the week and confirm the ceremony start time.
- Make sure guests can distinguish the ceremony location from the reception location.
- Test any website address you include.
- Check the trimmed proof fits your chosen envelope.
