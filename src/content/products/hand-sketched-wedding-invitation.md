This is the quietest design in the shop, and deliberately so. A drawn venue illustration carries the whole card, and the typography stays out of its way.

## What you get

- An editable Canva template
- A print-ready 5x7 layout with bleed
- The Canva Basics guide with video tutorials
- Instant download after purchase

## Who it suits

Formal weddings, and anyone who wants the invitation to look designed rather than decorated. There is no floral border, no watercolor wash, and no script font doing three jobs at once.

## Printing

Fine linework wants a matte or cotton stock. Glossy finishes fight the drawing. Weights and printer suggestions are in the [printing guide](/printing-guide/).

## Wording

If you need help with the wording itself — hosting lines, divorced parents, dress code phrasing — the [wording and etiquette guide](/wording-etiquette/) covers the common situations.
