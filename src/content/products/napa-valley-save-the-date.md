## A vineyard save the date that helps guests make a plan

This Napa Valley save the date template pairs a hand-drawn vineyard sketch with editable wording on the front and back. The illustration introduces your wine country setting; the reverse gives you room for the practical information guests need before the formal invitation arrives.

Use the gallery to review both sides. The design can suit a Napa wedding or another wine country celebration when you change the location line. The existing illustration remains the same, so choose it for the scenery and style rather than expecting a new drawing of your venue.

## Keep the front simple and useful

Your names, wedding date, and destination are the essentials. Add “formal invitation to follow” to make the card’s purpose clear. Use the actual town or location that helps guests plan; a broad regional name alone may not tell them where to stay.

Here is a fictional wording example to adapt:

> Save the date\
> Taylor and Alex are getting married\
> [Month, day, year]\
> [Town], California\
> Formal invitation to follow\
> [Wedding website]

Proofread the date after every edit, especially if you change the font or line breaks. For hosting language and more destination examples, visit the [save the date wording guide](/wording-etiquette/).

## Use the back for the next step guests should take

The reverse is most helpful when it answers a real planning question. Add the wedding website, a short travel note, and accommodation information only if it is confirmed. Avoid filling the card with a schedule that may change.

For example:

> We can’t wait to celebrate with you in wine country.\
> Accommodation suggestions and travel details: [Website]\
> More information will be added as plans are confirmed.

If a room block is ready, include the correct booking link or direct guests to the page that explains it. If it is still being arranged, say when guests can expect an update on the website. Do not describe accommodation as reserved until that is accurate.

## Personalize and export in five steps

1. Open the Canva template through the files supplied with your Etsy purchase.
2. Replace names, date, and destination on the front.
3. Add your website and a concise, confirmed travel note on the back.
4. Check both sides for consistent spelling, spacing, and readable text.
5. Export a PDF for printing or a JPEG for digital sharing.

Test your website on a phone before including it. If sharing a JPEG, add the website as a clickable link in the message as well; guests should not have to retype a small URL from an image.

## Print both sides without surprises

Tell the printer this is a two-sided 5x7-inch card and ask how they want the front and back supplied. Follow their guidance on page order, bleed, and crop marks. A printed proof lets you check orientation, trimming, color, and the smallest text before committing to the full quantity.

For a home print, test the duplex orientation on plain paper first. Then use cardstock supported by your printer and check that the exported file prints at the intended size. Compare the finished proof with your envelope measurements. The [printing guide](/printing-guide/) covers file preparation and paper choices.

## Choose a mailing date around your guests’ travel needs

Confirm your date, destination, and intended guest list before sending save the dates. For a celebration that requires travel, an eight-to-twelve-month planning window is a useful starting point. Adjust it to your booking timeline and how much notice your guests need.

You do not need every weekend detail finalized before sending. You do need a reliable date, a useful destination, and a clear place for guests to find updates. Keep evolving information on your website so the printed card remains useful.
