Wine country weddings are usually a weekend, not an afternoon. Guests drive up, stay two nights, and want to know which town to book in. That makes the back of a save the date more useful than the front, and this one is designed with that in mind.

## What you get

- An editable Canva template, front and back
- A print-ready 5x7 layout with bleed
- The Canva Basics guide with video tutorials
- Instant download after purchase

## The back matters here

There is space for a hotel block, the nearest town, a wedding website, and a short note about travel. Guests booking a Napa weekend are making a real plan, and answering the questions early saves you a month of text messages.

## When to send

Eight to twelve months out. Napa hotels during harvest fill early and get expensive, so the earlier your guests can book, the more of them will actually come.
